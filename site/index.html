Guide only. The coach or captain makes the call. Look at the water before going out.

Updated about hourly from BOM, Melbourne Water and Open-Meteo. "Lights to/from" under a session shows when boat lights are needed. Most conditions on their own mean caution. These combinations mean no go: dark + fog, dark + rain, rain + wind, outgoing tide + high river.
