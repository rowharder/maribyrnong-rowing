About

This is a guide only. The coach or captain on the day makes the call. Always look at the water before going out.

Updated about every hour from the Bureau of Meteorology (forecasts, observations, warnings, tides), Melbourne Water river gauges and Open-Meteo hourly forecasts. Darkness or an outgoing tide alone means caution, and light rain alone is fine. Some combinations are dangerous and turn red: dark + fog, dark + rain, rain + wind, and an outgoing tide on a high river.
